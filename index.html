How Do I Get There? – London Directions

Short Instructions (Pair Work)

  • Start at Buckingham Palace.
  • Choose a destination.
  • Student A asks: “How do I get to …?”
  • Student B gives directions.
  • Look at the map and describe the route.
  • Click the useful expressions to add them to the box.
  • Change roles and choose another place.

Starting point: Buckingham Palace (FIXED)

Useful expressions (A1–B1)

Asking for directions
  • Excuse me, where is …?
  • How do I get to …?
  • Can you tell me the way to …?
  • Is it far from here?
  • Which way is …?
  • Could you point me towards …?
  • Is this the right way to …?
  • How long does it take to walk there?
  • Can you show me on the map?
  • Is it near here?
Giving directions
  • Go straight ahead.
  • Turn left / right.
  • Walk past the …
  • Cross the street.
  • You’ll see it on your left / right.
  • Keep going until you reach …
  • Walk through the park.
  • Follow the signs for …
  • It’s next to the …
  • Stay on this road.
